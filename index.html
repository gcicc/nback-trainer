How N-Back Works

1
A square lights up in the 3x3 grid. A new square lights up every 2.5 seconds.
2
Your job: tap MATCH if the current square is in the same position as the one from N steps ago.
3
In 2-Back mode, you compare to 2 steps ago. In 3-Back, three steps ago. The higher the N, the harder.
4
If it's a match and you tap MATCH → Hit!
If it's a match and you don't tap → Miss.
If it's not a match and you tap → False alarm.
5
Start with 2-Back. When you consistently hit 80%+ accuracy, move up a level.
